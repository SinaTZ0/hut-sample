The Central Library of Hamedan University of Technology was established at the same time as the establishment of the university in 1385 with the aim of collecting scientific and educational resources and disseminating the information needed by students and professors, in order to strengthen the educational and research programs of the university. This library is located on the ground floor of the university building.

The software used in the library is Simorgh software, which library members can use to search for needed resources, and reserve or renew books, through the computers located in the library or the university website, or through the Internet at simorgh.hut.ac .ir, use this software.
In the entrance section of the library, there is a box called the book return box so that members can put their returned books in it during non-office hours. The return of the books in the library software will be on the next working day.
The creativity and innovation center  in order to foster the emergence of creativity and promote innovation by using the capacities and facilities available in universities, research institutes and science and technology parks and in line with the law of the 5-year economic, social and cultural development programs of the Islamic Republic of Iran. And following the notification of executive regulations, they are established. In fact, these centers play the role of fostering, consulting, setting goals, planning and directing students' raw ideas to achieve a successful and productive business. For this purpose, on 6/2/1995 and in the presence of the Honorable Vice President of Research and Technology of the Ministry of Science, Research and Technology, a cooperation memorandum was signed between Hamedan University of Technology and Science and Technology Park of Hamedan Province to set up a center for the flourishing of creativity and innovation of Hamedan University of Technology.
In 2019, this center was upgraded to the innovation center of Hamadan University of Technology and its location was moved to the fifth floor of the central building of the university, and the stages of equipping and preparing to provide the best services to the teams that are members and active in this center were successfully completed. Equipping and setting up central laboratories and creating infrastructure for applied basic research is one of the strategic goals of Hamedan University of Technology. According to the report of the public relations office of the university, the central laboratory of Hamedan University of Technology has been set up in two levels A and B since 2015. As an important center, this collection aims to meet the research needs of researchers not only at the university and provincial level but also at the national level.
The central laboratory of Hamedan University of Technology in 2015 in line with the realization of the strategic document of the university approved on 04/31/2016 with the aim of providing research services to faculty members, researchers and graduate students of the university, researchers and industrialists of the country as well as providing services to institutions outside The university was established.
Other duties of this laboratory include trying to concentrate and organize the advanced facilities and equipment needed by different disciplines, optimal efficiency of the existing facilities, saving the cost of projects, conducting research in a path with minimal challenges. He mentioned creating more communication among researchers and transferring information, access of faculty members, researchers and students to research spaces and facilities, and avoiding monopolization of devices and equipment.
The central laboratory is divided into two levels, A and B. Level A equipment includes Hi-Tech equipment and Level B equipment includes general laboratory equipment.
The services of the central laboratory are included in all universities, scientific and research institutions, various industries and also the private sector. Therefore, all natural and legal persons can benefit from its services by visiting the laboratory or by calling. Among the goals of this laboratory, the following can be mentioned. Establishing communication between laboratories inside and outside the university in order to provide mutual services, optimal use of the research power of faculty members, improving the educational and research quality of graduate students, improving the quality and variety of services to customers, and increasing the satisfaction of establishing communication between the university and industry through By sharing scientific and laboratory facilities, increasing compliance with the principles of safety, workplace health and environmental regulations according to standards.
